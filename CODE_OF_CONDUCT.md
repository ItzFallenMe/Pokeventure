# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

This project is a hobby fan game. Everyone is welcome to use it, report bugs,
suggest ideas and contribute, regardless of experience, background, identity or
skill level. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for
everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique code, not people.
- Keep discussion on topic: the app, the server, the documentation.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution that does not fit the
  project's direction, scope or licence.
- Respect the Pokémon trademark and do not push the project toward commercial
  use, paid content or app-store redistribution.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Sexualised content or attention, and content involving minors.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, sustained disruption, or bad-faith issue and pull-request flooding.
- Sharing leaked keys, private server addresses or other people's data.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **vedantyadav196@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, or
block accounts, depending on severity. Good-faith reports will never be
retaliated against.
